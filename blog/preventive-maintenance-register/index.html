A maintenance register that makes downtime visible

Link each asset to its service history, approved maintenance schedule and operational impact.

## Give each asset an identity A scanner, printer or conveyor needs a stable asset reference before its failure history becomes useful. Record location, responsible owner, manufacturer guidance and service contacts. Keep credentials and access codes out of the register. ## Plan from the approved requirement Use manufacturer instructions and competent maintenance advice to define checks and service intervals. This is a planning record, not a substitute for technical training or site safety procedures. Assign maintenance work only to authorised personnel. ## Capture the interruption Record failure start, restoration time, symptom, action and parts used. Separate time spent waiting for assistance from actual repair time. Note the affected process so the business can see why a small device failure caused a large queue. ## Review recurring issues Compare repeated symptoms, spare availability and missed scheduled checks. Decide whether the next improvement is maintenance, replacement, training or a change in usage.
