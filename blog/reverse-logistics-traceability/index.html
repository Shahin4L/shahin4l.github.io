Reverse logistics: keep identity and custody connected

Connect the return request, collected item, physical condition and final disposition in one auditable journey.

## The return has its own journey Forward delivery completion does not describe what happens during a return. Give each return a unique reference linked to the original order where applicable. Keep the reason, collection event and destination decision visible. ## Preserve the evidence chain At each handover, record item identity, quantity, condition and receiving responsibility. Use an approved inspection procedure; opening, testing and disposal rights depend on the product and agreement. Do not assume every collected item is automatically fit for resale. ## Separate movement from disposition An item may be collected, received, awaiting inspection or approved for a specific next step. Record these separately. A return is not resolved merely because it reached the warehouse; refund, repair or stock decisions may still be outstanding. ## Start with a sample Trace ten returns from request to final recorded disposition. List every missing identifier, timestamp or owner. Fix the most common gap first and monitor unresolved return age without repeatedly restarting its clock. ## Further reading [GS1 — Barcode Standards](https://www2.gs1.org/standards/barcodes) *Practical editorial guide.
