A reliable inbound handover in four checks

Make quantity, identity, condition and custody visible before incoming work enters the next process.

## A handover transfers responsibility A vehicle arrival timestamp is not the same as a completed receipt. Record arrival, unloading start and accepted receipt as separate events so that waiting and processing time remain visible. ## Four checks to standardise 1. Quantity: compare expected and physically counted handling units. 2. Identity: reconcile labels and manifest references. 3. Condition: record visible damage or tampering using the approved process. 4. Custody: record who transferred and who accepted responsibility. ## Handle discrepancies openly If a manifest lists 50 containers and 49 arrive, accept the verified quantity and open a discrepancy record for the missing unit. Do not make the system match by entering an unverified receipt. Store supporting evidence with access limited to the people resolving the issue. ## Review the queue Separate received-and-ready items from disputed or unreadable items. Give every exception an owner and next update time. Monitor waiting time before unloading as well as time spent receiving; either can hold up the downstream shift. ## Further reading [Lean Enterprise Institute — Standardized Work](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/standardized-work/) *Practical editorial guide.
