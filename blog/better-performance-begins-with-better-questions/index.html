Better Performance Begins with Better Questions.

Better performance starts with better questions. Explore how clear expectations, timely feedback, employee accountability and organizational support work together to build stronger teams.

Better Performance Begins with Better Questions. When performance falls below expectations, our first instinct is often to evaluate the individual. But what if we also evaluated the environment in which they are expected to perform? In operations, continuous improvement begins with understanding the root cause, not simply judging the outcome. The same principle can be applied to people development. Before drawing conclusions, it is worth asking: • Are expectations clear? • Are the right tools and training available? • Is feedback timely and constructive? • Are employees taking ownership of their own development? As technology evolves and business expectations grow, both individual capabilities and organizational support need to evolve. Employee accountability and organizational support are not competing responsibilities. They complement each other. The purpose of performance management should not be limited to measuring results. It should also include creating opportunities for better results. Perhaps one of the most valuable leadership practices is to ask not only, "How is this person performing?" but also, "How can we help this person perform better?" Because when people grow, organizations grow with them. Don't just evaluate performance.
