Cycle counting: investigate the difference before adjusting stock

Use controlled counts and discrepancy reviews to improve the reliability of inventory records.

## Counting is a control, not a cleanup Cycle counting checks selected stock regularly instead of waiting for a full annual count. Choose a frequency based on business importance, movement and discrepancy history. The aim is a trustworthy record and fewer recurring errors. ## Control the counting window Identify the location and item clearly. Freeze movements briefly or keep a reliable transaction cut-off. Where practical, let the first counter work without seeing the expected quantity, then independently recount material differences. ## Follow the transaction trail If the system shows 120 units and the count shows 116, examine recent receipts, picks, transfers and unit-of-measure changes before posting an adjustment. Four missing units and four unposted picks require different corrective actions. ## Close with learning Require approval for adjustments according to business policy. Record the reason, evidence and corrective action. Track exact-match locations and repeated discrepancy causes; a high adjustment volume can make balances look tidy while the underlying process remains unreliable. ## Further reading [Lean Enterprise Institute — Standardized Work](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/standardized-work/) *Practical editorial guide.
