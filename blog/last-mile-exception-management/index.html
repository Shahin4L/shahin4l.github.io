Manage last-mile exceptions with clear next actions

Replace vague delivery failure labels with specific reasons, evidence and a realistic follow-up.

## A status is not a resolution A delivery marked unsuccessful still needs a next step. Distinguish an address issue, customer rescheduling, access restriction and an internal capacity problem. A vague "customer unavailable" bucket hides different recovery needs. ## Design an exception record Keep the parcel reference, attempt time, approved reason, relevant evidence and agreed next action. Restrict customer details to the operational team. Never expose phone numbers or precise addresses in a management screenshot shared outside that group. ## Plan an honest recovery A requested reschedule should have a confirmed time window and responsible team. An unclear address needs verification before another dispatch. A capacity problem needs a staffing or routing decision, rather than repeated customer calls. ## Check for repeated failure Review items with multiple unsuccessful attempts and audit a sample of reason codes. Compare successful delivery with eligible attempts under a documented definition. Do not improve the percentage by removing difficult parcels after the fact. ## Further reading [ASQ — Plan-Do-Check-Act](https://asq.org/quality-resources/pdca-cycle) *Practical editorial guide.
