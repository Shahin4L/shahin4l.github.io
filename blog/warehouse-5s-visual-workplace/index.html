5S in a warehouse: make the correct action obvious

Use workplace organisation to reduce searching and reveal abnormal conditions during everyday work.

## Organisation must support the work A clean warehouse can still be difficult to operate. Use 5S to make needed tools, approved locations and abnormal conditions easy to recognise. Begin with a small station rather than repainting the whole building. ## Start at the point of use Remove unneeded items through an approved review. Give frequently used tools a clearly identified home. Keep labels consistent with the system and separate normal stock, damaged items and unresolved exceptions. ## Test the arrangement Observe a normal work cycle before and after the change. Record search time, unnecessary walking and misplaced items. A visual label that workers cannot read from their usual position needs revision, even if it looks attractive in a photograph. ## Sustain through ownership Name a station owner and include a short reset at shift handover. Let workers report why the arrangement fails under peak demand. Treat repeated disorder as a design problem to investigate, not automatic evidence of poor discipline. ## Further reading [Lean Enterprise Institute — 5S](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/five-s/) *Practical editorial guide.
