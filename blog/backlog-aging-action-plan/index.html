Turn backlog aging into a recovery plan

Use age, service risk and ownership together to prevent an aging report from becoming a passive list.

## Age needs a clear clock A parcel can look old for different reasons. Measure journey age from the agreed starting event and stage age from entry into the current stage. A routine status edit should not silently restart either clock. ## Make a recovery queue Group overdue work by location, stage and reason. Prioritise service commitments, customer impact and items that can move now. Give blocked items a separate queue with a named dependency owner. ## Keep the balance honest Suppose the overdue queue opens at 100, gains 30 newly overdue items and resolves 40. It should close at 90, before documented scope adjustments. Moving an item to another hub is not automatically a customer resolution. ## Review the right evidence Track new overdue entries as well as old cases cleared. A falling total can hide new failures. During the shift review, check the oldest unresolved item and sample a few claimed resolutions against physical movement or delivery evidence. ## Further reading [Lean Enterprise Institute — Value Stream Mapping](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/value-stream-mapping/) *Practical editorial guide.
