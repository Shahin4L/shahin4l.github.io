WHEN A FORMULA DOESN’T WORK

Small details that save a lot of time.

Why does #NAME? appear?

Check spelling, function language and your Excel version. XLOOKUP, FILTER and UNIQUE need modern Excel; TEXTSPLIT and TAKE need 365/2024. Use a listed alternative where available.

Why do dates or monthly totals fail?

Test =ISNUMBER(D2). Dates must be real date values, not text. The Copy Sample button inserts DATE formulas. When importing CSV, explicitly set column D to Date.

Why does #SPILL! appear?

Clear the destination cells, unmerge any cells in the spill area and use dynamic-array formulas outside Excel Tables.

Why are commas rejected?

Some locales use semicolons as argument separators and different decimal conventions. Adjust separators for your spreadsheet locale; do not change punctuation inside quoted text blindly.

Rows or unique orders?

The sample deliberately repeats SO-105. Raw totals are 6 rows, 51 units and 5950 amount. Removing identical duplicate rows gives 5 orders, 39 units and 4450 amount. Confirm your reporting definition first.

How do I copy formulas correctly?

Keep source data in A:H on the Data sheet. Test in J2 or another empty area. $A$2 fixes both row and column; A$2 fixes only the row; $A2 fixes only the column.

Examples are educational, use fixed sample data and include no employer or customer records. Expected arithmetic and filtering results are checked independently; your app/version and locale can affect formula availability.
